# Trilobit Design Document Update 0.18

Work done while Eric was away.  Rebuild the world after updating (delete `cache`, make a new world).

## Code debt
`trilobit-tools/build_all.py` runs every generator in dependency order (dry run unless `--run`; `--only`, `--from`).  `common.py` holds shared helpers.  Resident/ambient code is now monster-based (earlier update).

## Guns (Milestone 22)
50 new guns from seven makers (see "Trilobit Gun List"), generated by `gen_gunset.py`; stand-in guns renamed (HP-8 Shinobi, SM-5 Kagura, GA-4 Marshal, GA-4S Garrison).  Faction loadouts and shop stock use them.

## Vehicles (Milestone 23)
`gen_fleet.py` adds 31 electric vehicles (bikes, trike, pods, wagons, sports cars, crossover, vans, pickups, trucks, buses, tow truck, fire engine, armored transport) by rebuilding stock designs: engines become electric motors, fuel tanks become battery banks.  With the earlier 12, there are 43.  Vehicles longer than 7 tiles or wider than 4 are not placed on streets or lots (they do not fit the lot mapgens); they can be spawned from debug or used for future depots.  NPC driving is not possible in CDDA without a large C++ driver system, so it is not done.

## Tilesets and icons
Three alternatives to Bouma, same layout and ids: **Neonoir** (teal/magenta night, bloom), **Pixel16** (16-colour dithered), **Smog** (hazy, desaturated, neon stays vivid).  All include the upgraded people (coats, hair styles, cyber eyes, hoods; android with faceplate, visor, joints and chest light) and about 1,350 new item icons (`icons3.py`; generic gems fell from 1,483 to about 100, mostly pseudo-items and real gemstones).  Select in Options > Graphics.

## Enemy AI (steps 6-10, partial)
Implemented in `src/trl_aware.cpp` plus `gen_groupai.py`:
- F3 group alarm: a creature that starts hunting shouts; aware same-faction monsters within 14 tiles come to the spot.
- F12 morale: an aware monster below 25% hp breaks and runs.
- F8 disguise: wearing a Bratva coat, NAP patrol jacket or Nasim hoodie (sold in shops) slows that faction's guards to notice you beyond two tiles.
- F9/F13: hostile script-hacker monster that launches hunter drones (spell).
Not done: cover seeking and tactics (F5), patrol routes beyond lot patrols (F7), surrender.  The enemy types (thug, soldier, sniper, assassin, cyborg, hacker) are not yet placed in the world by any generator.
