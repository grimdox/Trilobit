ART GUARDRAILS (in addition to CORE RULES)
 - 100% original art, generated in code (trilobit-tools/gen_tiles64.py,
   t64_* modules, art64.Art) into gfx/Trilobit64, then
   `godot --headless --path . --import`. No traced, copied or
   "in the style of <named artwork>" output. No known characters, logos,
   brands, signage with real names, or real-world text.
 - Readability first: at 64 px the thing must be identifiable at a glance,
   walkable vs blocking must be clear, and it must not be confused with a
   neighbouring tile type.
 - Grit on every tile: wear, grime, patches, stains. Nothing pristine except
   upper-district glass, and even that gets a smudge/reflection.
 - Trilobit64 = rich cyberpunk palette, drawn in code by the t64_*.py
   modules.  Everything must be original: no sprites, ids or data taken from
   CDDA or any other game.
 - Sizes follow the SCALE list exactly (tile multiples). Multi-tile sprites
   align to the 64 px grid and rotate/layer like the existing vehicles
   (3 painted layers).
 - Colour-blind safe: do not rely on red vs green alone for danger/loot.
 - Faction colours stay consistent with what is already in the game
   (check existing tiles/sprites before choosing).
 - UI: authored at 1280x720 and multiplied by UI_S (1.5). Text must stay
   legible; no text baked into sprites.
 - Performance: no per-frame generation; art is baked to files.
