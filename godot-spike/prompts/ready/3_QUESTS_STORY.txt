    street-level scum.

STORY GUARDRAILS (in addition to CORE RULES)
 - Original names, places and plot. No borrowed characters, lines or
   settings. Mood references only.
 - Use the established structure: main lines have 7 beats and a choice that
   changes the city; side trees have ~4 beats run by a secondary NPC; beats
   map to hooks the engine has (dialogue topics, spawn NPC, go-to location,
   map markers, faction rank). Standing gates: beats 1-2 Neutral, 3-5 Known,
   6-7 Trusted.
 - Every quest offers at least two real approaches (e.g. fight / stealth /
   talk / hack), with consequences, and at least one non-violent route.
 - Betrayal is always possible and always remembered: opposing faction gains
   standing, betrayed faction turns hostile in its districts.
 - Faction triangle: helping one raises the other two's suspicion a step.
 - Rank changes use the Defaults table (kill member -2 / +1 to its enemies;
   quest +1 or +2 and -1 with main enemy; robbing/hacking -1; crimes seen -1).
 - Rewards: yen, rank, faction gear, or a unique item. Amounts must be
   compared to existing prices/jobs (state the comparison).
 - Quests are given by NAMED NPCs, not terminals. Terminals/fixers handle
   generic jobs (courier, retrieval).
 - Respect pillar 3: a fully human PC must be able to finish every quest or be
   offered a human route; implants/hacking add options, never gate main lines.
 - Writing quality: short, concrete, specific to place and person; dialogue
   sounds like a person (verbal habits, wants, secrets); no lore dumps, no
   "As you know", no chosen-one cliches, no real-world slurs or brands.
 - Items/guns: obey the guns rule (RU/CN/JP/US + the 25 originals), the
   melee rule, no "makeshift"/"broken" items, stable ids.
