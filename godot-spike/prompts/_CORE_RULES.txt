PILLARS (never break these)
 1. Many ways to fight: stealth OR open combat must both stay viable.
 2. Death is permanent; ghosts can move between shells.
 3. Staying human is a choice. Fully human characters get no cyberware and
    cannot be hacked.
 4. Open world with a way out. Two cities in one: gleaming high-rises vs
    street-level scum.
 5. Tone: gritty, stylized, grit on every tile. Mood references only (Ghost in
    the Shell, Anarchy Online): NO names, art, characters, logos, brands or
    real-world text copied from anything. Originality is required.

FIXED FACTS (do not rename or renumber)
 - Currency: yen. One turn = one minute.
 - Factions: Bratva (id trl_bratva), NAP (trl_nap), Nasim (trl_nasim); also
   CIV and GANG in the sim. Ranks -10..+10. IDs never change.
   Bratva = Russian-style syndicate (low districts, debts, trucks, family).
   NAP = Japanese/American-style police-corporate force. Nasim = hacker/zealot
   sect (hostile to implanted PCs below rank +7).
 - Guns: ONLY real-style guns made in Russia, China, Japan, America, plus the
   25 original guns in the Gun List. Melee: Japanese or Russian blades/arms.
   No "makeshift", "improvised" or "broken" items.
 - Nanohacks cost Control Value (CV). Cyberbrain Mk I-V give CV limits
   4/8/14/22/32; hack costs 1-6.
 - Androids = frame + role + modules + firmware.
 - Scale: lane 6 tiles, road 12, cars 4x6, trucks 6x12, helicopter 6x9,
   plane 12x10, mech 5x5, train 4 wide x 6-long carriages x4. Tiles 64 px;
   canvas 1920x1080; UI authored 1280x720 and scaled 1.5x (UI_S).
 - Tilesets: Bouma (default, classic cyberpunk) and Mokuhanga (black/white/neon
   print look, at most 3 inks). Readability first.
