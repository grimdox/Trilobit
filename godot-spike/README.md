# Trilobit Godot spike

A time-boxed test of whether Godot 4 can carry Trilobit instead of CDDA.  Three pass/fail tests, run headless with `./run_all.sh`
(Godot 4.3, GDScript only, no C++).  Plan, results and what the spike does not prove are in the Project doc "Trilobit Godot Spike Plan".

| Test | Files |
|---|---|
| 1. NPC scale with level of detail | `src/flowfield.gd`, `src/npc_sim.gd`, `tests/test_scale.gd` |
| 2. Pocket inventory | `src/item.gd`, `src/item_db.gd`, `tests/test_pockets.gd`, `tools/export_items.py` |
| 3. Anchored overworld and a city | `src/worldgen.gd`, `src/citygen.gd`, `data/anchors.json`, `tests/test_world.gd` |

## Keys added in round three
`<` / PgUp go up, `>` / PgDn go down (stairs, elevators, ladders; an elevator rides three floors), `t` talk, `p` pay 100 on the debt (Shift+P 1000).  Walk into a locked door to pick it; step onto a carriage while the train doors are open to board.
## Movement and stealth
`c` crouch, `z` crawl, Shift+direction run, `q` then a direction jump.  People look in a cone and hear your steps by stance; strike unaware people from behind for a quiet kill; use crates, dumpsters, planters and railings as cover.

## Levels, implants and melee
`<` `>` stairs and elevators (or, with jump jets, fly up and down); `e` gets into gyros (flying) and mechs; `q` + direction jumps, off an edge you fall; `w` + direction strikes at reach; `\\` swaps weapon; `o` + direction shoves people, crates, tables, chairs and animals (or just walk into a crate to push it).  Ripperdocs sell leg actuators and jump jets.  25 Japanese and Russian melee weapons in three grades.

## Guns
`f` fire at the target (Shift+F at anyone), Tab cycle targets, `a` aim (stacks to 3 turns), `r` reload or clear a jam, `v` fire mode, `x` next ammunition kind, `F4` gunsmith (fit scopes, suppressors, magazines, grips, stocks, barrels).

## Round five: the island, scale, menus, combat
- **World**: `src/island.gd` generates a 2304x1408 island from a seed (west harbor, one mountain range with a thin winding pass carrying a 12-wide highway and a 4-wide railway, the city, a small town, plains and fields, a sheer east cliff, rocky north and south shores).  The six-lane (36-wide) highway runs from the harbor quay through the city and on east to the clifftop, narrowing to twelve only inside the pass; a river rises in the mountains, runs through the city and out to the south coast.  It is a 5x3 grid of 512x512 zones that overlap, except the city zone, which is 1024x1024 (`ISL.CZS`, origin `CITY_ORIGIN`, territory `CITY_OWN`); `src/world.gd` keeps one zone Game live and swaps zones at the border, carrying the player and any vehicle.  `M` opens the island map with a you-are-here marker.
- **Vehicles** (`src/vehicles.gd`): cars 4x6 (2x2 cabin, engine bay, trunk), trucks, helicopters, planes, mechs; engines swap (`Shift+E` / `F6`), parts take damage; NPCs drive.  Streets are 12 wide (two lanes of six), bridge avenues 24 (four lanes), the highway 36 (six lanes), rail corridors 4 wide.
- **Menus**: title, options (rebind keys, sound), Esc pause with save slots, load, implants body map (`F3`).
- **Action points and melee**: everyone (player, NPCs, animals) earns 100 AP a turn, scaled by stamina % (cats 110, reflex wires more); an action costs 100, so a tired body acts less often.  Running costs 5 stamina a move, a swing 1 per kg of weapon, regeneration is 1 + 1 per 2 Grit.  A blow is 2d10 + Melee/Unarmed - target Dodge (11+ hits; natural 19-20 or 20 + Grit crits; natural 2 or less fumbles and gives a free swing), lands on torso 30% / head 10% / each limb 15%, and does weapon roll + skill - armor - Grit (a crit doubles weapon + skill at maximum roll); damage past a limb wears its implants.  The HUD stamina bar shows your AP a turn.  `tests/test_ap.gd`.
- **Ranged**: a shot costs 50 AP and 2 stamina, aiming 100 AP and 1.  Each bullet rolls 2d10 + Ranged skill + banked aim + implants/stance - the distance between the target and a roll of the gun's natural range, times the cover factor (11+ hits; natural 19-20 or 20 + Grit crits; natural 2 or less fumbles and drops your aim), lands on the same body parts as melee and does bullet roll + skill - armor - Grit.  Aiming banks points up to the gun's Max Aim; every bullet fired costs aim (bursts bottom out at Max Aim - 5).  Guns carry no damage: the loaded bullet does (damage range and effective range per bullet type, losing 1/effective-range of its damage for every tile past it).  Every gun shows natural range, max aim, clip and mod slots, and the loaded bullet's damage, on the gunsmith screen; gunmen spend an action raising the gun before they fire (the tell).  `tests/test_ranged.gd`.
- **NPC body parts**: every person has your six limbs (scaled to their health), hit by the same location rolls.  Blows and bullets wound the limb they land on as well as overall health; a destroyed head or chest kills, a disabled arm loses the weapon hand (one arm: worse aim, none: no attacks), a disabled leg halves dodge and doubles step cost.  The log announces disabled limbs and the look cursor shows wounds.  `tests/test_npcparts.gd`.
- **Combat**: per-limb health (`src/body.gd`; head/chest at 0 is death, other limbs go offline), flat-deduction armor on worn items and implants that wears out, ranged accuracy from sights/scope/brain and eye implants, aim speed, cover, bullet dodge only from grade 3+ implants, melee accuracy/attack speed/dodge, parry and riposte hooks (`melee_arts`).  HP, armor and a body diagram are drawn top right.
Tests: `./run_all.sh` (pockets, world, scale, levels, game, vertical, guns, stealth, zmove, melee, npcfall, things, menus, vehicles, island, combat, ap, ranged, npcparts).  Screenshots: `xvfb-run -a godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 -s tests/shot3.gd`.

## Round six
- Fixed a crash when stepping onto the railway in any zone without a subway (`transit.car_cell` took a modulo by an empty ring).
- Exe icon (the splash-screen car, `tools/content/gen_icon.py`) and version info are stamped by `tools/set_exe_icon.py`; `tools/build_windows.sh` exports, stamps and zips.
- Loading screen shows とりろびつ (bundled kana subset of Noto Sans CJK JP, OFL); the title tag line is gone; menus take mouse hover, click, right-click back and wheel.
- Everyone has an on-screen facing arrow (people, animals, you); the drawn vision-cone tint is removed.  The player sees a 180 degree half circle (`vision_arc()`; implants may add degrees with an `arc` field); `;` + direction turns without a step.  NPCs still look back at you all round (`fov.around`).
- Windows SmartScreen only goes away with a trusted code-signing certificate; see README-FIRST.txt in the zip for the workaround.

## City layout, districts and the harbor
- The city's blocks are merged into bigger buildings: mega-towers fill 2-3 blocks each way (4-9 blocks) in the centre, the ring around them has buildings 1-2 blocks wide (1-4 blocks), and houses/venues sit in the outer suburbs.  Mega-towers are always among the tallest.
- Wealth districts (`CityGen.wealth`, lower / middle / upper): bare concrete and dingy tint, plain, or marble and bright tint; venue tier, residents' cash and clothes follow the district.  The HUD shows the district name.
- The harbor: north end = unaffiliated criminals (faction GANG) around derelict warehouses, centre = the working port (quay road with AI trucks, six animated gantry cranes, container yard, dock workers in hard hats), south end = Bratva around the dormitories.
- `tools/content/gen_tiles64.py` draws the whole 64x64 tileset (terrain, furniture, harbor props, items, animals, top-down paper dolls); `tools/build_windows.sh` runs it.

## Rail line, side panel, hunger and thirst
- One rail line, three trains in a repeating loop (`transit.gd`).  Tracks are two tiles wide and the line is two-track (four rows apart); a train is four tiles wide (wall/door lane, two floor lanes you can stand and walk in, wall/door lane) and each carriage is six tiles long, four carriages to a train; the two middle tiles of each side wall are the doors, which open at stops.  The route: the four city stations (level -1, 30-tile platforms on both sides of the train), a twin-track tunnel west to a portal, the island railway through the pass, the Harbor Terminal (platform on the port siding), buffers, and back on the return track.  Trains are stateless (a tabulated timetable indexed by a clock that travels with the player), so every zone game draws just its part of the line; riders carry across the zone border.  Step onto a carriage while its doors are open to board, onto a platform to leave.
- The right-hand side panel (`view.gd _draw_hud`) shows place/time/yen, an empty NAV IMPLANT bay (the minimap is a purchasable upgrade, not built yet), an anatomical figure tinted per limb, limb meters with armour, health/armour/stamina, food and water meters, status effects, speed, heat, carry weight and the gun.
- **Display** (Options > Visuals): windowed (at a chosen size: 1280x720 up to 3840x2160, never bigger than the screen), borderless fullscreen, or exclusive fullscreen.  F11 or Alt+Enter flips windowed and fullscreen from any screen (both are listed on the key list); remembered in settings.  `--fullscreen` starts that way.  The window title and the title and loading screens read とりろびつ.
- **Keys**: `?` opens the key bindings list (the help screen is gone); `L` is look (it left east; the other vi keys are unchanged), `Enter` the wait menu, `F8` or `'` consume.  The key list keeps each action name and its keys in their own columns.
- **The clock**: a turn is six seconds (10 turns = 1 min, 600 = 1 h, 14,400 = 1 day; `src/clock.gd`).  The HUD shows HH:MM:SS.  Clock-based timers (hunger and thirst, debt interest at midnight and the installments, the stabiliser, NPC schedules, the train's crowds, healing, heat decay, blessings, drink) all use it.  Saves from the minute-long turns are converted on load (`tv` key in saves).
- **Wait** (`Enter`): 1 turn, 10 turns (1 min), 100 turns (10 min), 600 turns (1 h), or until 6am, noon, 6pm or midnight.  It runs a few turns a frame and stops early when you are hurt, get hungry or thirsty, or a hostile you can see is on to you; `Esc` stops it.  `.` and `Space` still wait one turn.
- **Consume** (`F8`): food and drink in your pack or within one tile, and the water in a bottle.  Every food has `kcal` and every drink `ml` (and `hydr`, how much of it counts as water) in `data/items.json` (written by `tools/export_items.py`): 2,500 kcal = 24 h before you are Hungry, 500 mL of water = 4 h before you are Thirsty.  A full stomach holds exactly that much; the rest is wasted and the log says so.
- **Look** (`L`): a cursor over what you see (up to your sight range, Shift = 5 tiles); the view follows it; `Enter` writes what is there into the log; `L` or `Esc` ends.  Your sight range is 36 tiles (it was 18); `Game.sight_bonus()` is where implants and scopes will add to it.
- Hunger and thirst: 24 hours (14,400 turns) without food makes you Hungry, 4 hours (2,400 turns) without a drink makes you Thirsty; each is -20% walking speed (stacking to 64%).  `Game.last_ate`, `last_drank` are the clocks; `eat_now` moves them by `kcal_turns` / `ml_turns`.  Tests: `tests/run_timeui.gd` (77).

## Attributes and skills
- Five attributes (Strength, Perception, Agility, Cognition, Endurance), all 10 at the start.  Every two points over 10 add 1 to each skill the attribute governs.  Sixteen skills in four groups (Combat: Unarmed, Melee, Ranged, Dodge, Grit; Hacking: Networking, Analysis, Implementation; Labour: Manual, Engineering, Artisan, Research; Social: Streetwise, Etiquette, Deception, Negotiation), all 0 at the start, each raised by its own XP (`skill_up`: risky tasks teach, easy ones do not; base level capped at 20).  The number shown and used is the effective level (`Game.lvl(id)`) = base + attribute bonus.
- `src/skills.gd` holds the tables (names, governing attributes, descriptions, disciplines, whether the skill is live yet) and the shared formulas.  Disciplines unlock at an effective level: Parry 3, Riposte 6 (Melee), Iron Fist 5, Pressure Points 8 (Unarmed), Steady Aim 5, Marksman 8 (Ranged).
- In play today: Unarmed/Melee (+1 to the 2d10 roll and +1 damage a level), Ranged (+1 to the 2d10 roll a level, aim speed), Dodge (-1 to an attacker's melee roll a level; bullets only from level 7), Grit (-1 damage a level from blows and bullets, and a total of 20 + Grit is needed for a roll to crit; blasts still use the older -1 per 4 levels), Engineering (lock picking, fewer gun jams), Manual (heavy shoving), Streetwise/Etiquette/Negotiation (shop prices, fixer pay), Deception (pickpocketing), Research (extra rumours).  Hacking skills and Artisan wait for the nanohack and crafting systems (the screen says so).
- NPCs have the same five attributes and sixteen skills, derived from faction and person index (`SK.npc_attrs`, `SK.npc_base`; nothing stored, same on every replay of a seed).  They drive NPC dodge, bullet dodge, shooting and melee accuracy and Grit.
- **F7** (or Pause > Attributes & Skills) opens the screen: attributes in one box along the top, the selected line's description, governing attributes and disciplines beside it, four skill boxes below with the effective level and an XP bar.  Arrows, numpad or the mouse choose a line.
- Critical hits are new (decided by the defender's Grit, rolled by hashing the seed and turn so they never disturb the random stream); `Game.crits = false` switches them off for tests.
- Saves keep `attrs`, `skills` and `xp`; old saves load with the known skills kept and the rest at defaults.  Tests: `tests/test_skills.gd` (62 checks).  Screenshot: `tests/shot19.gd`.

**Character creation** (2026-10-18; `src/creation.gd`, screen in `screen_view.gd`, flow in `main.gd`): New Game (and `N` after death) opens one 1280x720 screen: 1 Appearance (the sprite only for now; skin, hair, style and eyes stay in the saved data without a row), 2 Background & Goals (a placeholder page), 3 Attributes (all start at 10; each 6 to 14; the total stays 50, so lowering one frees a point and a free point can only raise another; Begin needs an empty pool). Up/Down or W/S choose, Left/Right or A/D change, Tab jumps between blocks, Enter begins, Esc backs out; the mouse works on the arrows and buttons. The choice lives in `Game.app` and `Game.attrs`, is saved with the player and carried across zones; old saves load with the default figure. `--newgame` and `--seed=` still skip the screen. Test: `tests/run_creation.gd`.

## The city (revamp, 2026-10-17)
- **Shape**: the city zone's map is 1024x1024 and its territory is the 960x960 square `CITY_OWN` (a 32-tile border of the map belongs to the neighbours, as before), so one game holds the whole city.  The built-up area (`island.city_mask`) is a rounded square with a ragged edge, about 2.4x the 0.19 paved area, with 3x the building tiles (30% of the city is building); streets are kept only where they serve a block, so the grid stops where the town does.
- **River**: `island.river_tile` (tabulated per row from `RIVER_PTS`, so every zone agrees about it) rises inside the mountains at about (408, 238), leaves the foothills, runs through the west third of the city and reaches the south coast near row 1235, widening from about 13 tiles across (banks included) at the foothills to about 70 at the coast.  Sand banks, a two-tile quay (PLATFORM) along open banks, no building within two tiles of the water.
- **Bridges**: three four-lane avenues (24 wide, `bridges_y`, picked per seed: two north and one south of the highway, or the reverse) cross the river on decks with railings and dashed lane lines; the highway has its own six-lane deck and the railway its own railed deck beside it (five crossings in all).
- **Highway**: 36 wide (`HWY_N`/`HWY_S`, rows HWY_Y-18..+17) from the harbor's quay road, tapering to the old 12 in the pass (`hwy_wide`), through the city's middle row and east across the plains and the town (the town and airstrip moved clear of it) to the clifftop at x 2232.  The rails beside it moved 12 rows south where it is wide (`rail_ret(x)`, `rail_out(x)`).  Lane paint: yellow double centre, dashed white between lanes (`M_DASH_H`, mark 7).
- **Districts**: the downtown (DOWNTOWN, east of the river) has the mega-towers (up to 20 floors, `max_floors`), mid-rise blocks round it, then suburbs, then thin exurbs; the poor districts lie at the edges and on the west bank, the rich ones near the middle (`wealth`).
- **Rail**: the line leaves the daylight through the west tunnel mouth (local x 62), runs under the west bank, comes up at a second portal, crosses the river on the rail bridge (`island.rail_bridge`, `rail_portal`), goes down again into the downtown loop, which has four stations; the Harbor Terminal makes the fifth stop.  Trains are stateless and the path is a list of cells at z=0/-1, so the bridge needed only a longer path (`transit.build_line`).
- **Traffic**: AI cars understand roads of any width (`VS.lane_k2`, `VS.turn_lanes`; they turn right from the kerb lane and left from the median lane); a six-lane carriageway has three lanes each way.
- Tests: `tests/test_citylayout.gd` (31 checks).  Overview picture: `tests/probe_city.gd`.  Screenshots: `tests/shot20.gd -- out.png bridge|highway|rail`.

## Big seeded city (0.21)
- **Seeds**: `Island(seed)` jitters the river's course and width (`_seed_layout_river`) and picks the bridge rows, downtown and subway ring (`_seed_layout_city`) from its own RNG stream, so every new game (random seed; `--seed=N` repeats one) is a different map on the same themes.  The seed shows on the map screen.  `LAYOUT_VER` 3: older saves restart on the same island with a notice.
- **Size and density**: 1024x1024 map, pitch-54 streets, blocks from 30 tall, packed suburb rows, exurbs east of the river, 2000 people, 130 moving and 80 parked cars.
- **Boulevards**: curved 12-wide streets follow both banks (`island.boulevards()`); AI cars slide along the bends.
- **West Bank station**: platforms either side of the rail bridge's west end with stairs to the street; the line has seven stops.
- **Passengers**: commuters ride the carriages (stateless, a function of seed, train, cell and door-opening count; fuller at rush hours); the painted carriage sprites are `gfx/Trilobit64/veh/veh_train*.png`.
- **Island remake** (guide image): 4400x7750 tiles, 1 guide px = 4.4 tiles; see PLAN.md; `tests/test_remake.gd` (14).
- **Chart**: `overview_image(8)` draws the island from the seed (districts, bridges, ring, boulevards); `tests/test_bigcity.gd` (15).

## Clean of CDDA (2026-10-18)
- The game uses one tileset, **Trilobit64**, drawn by `tools/content/gen_tiles64.py`.  The Bouma and Mokuhanga sets (about 14,000 sprites keyed to CDDA ids) and the fallback that filled gaps from them are gone; every sprite the game draws is in Trilobit64 or the paper-doll sheet.  An item with no art of its own shows as a parcel.
- Guns: `tools/content/guns.py` is the only gun source (class table plus a row per gun: maker, weight, price, accuracy, damage, magazine, rarity, description).  `gen_gunset.py` and `data/source/gunset.json`, which built each gun on a CDDA base gun, are removed.  Stats in play are unchanged; descriptions are rewritten.
- Items no longer carry `looks_like` (CDDA sprite ids); sprites are found by item id.
- The removed files are kept, unchanged, on the `cdda-archive` branch.

## Standing paper dolls (2026-10-18)
- `tools/content/t64_figure.py` draws people standing (three-quarter turn, readied pose) as 64x64 layers that stack pixel for pixel (shadow, body, underwear, armor, arms, arm armor, held, hands) with five skin ramps (`skin_pale`, `skin_fair`, `skin_tan`, `skin_brown`, `skin_deep`); base body approved, not yet drawn in game; previews by `preview_figures.py` / `preview_skins.py`, checks by `check_figures.py` (in `run_all.sh`).
- Full standing-doll set (2026-10-18): `gen_figures.py` bakes `gfx/figure/fig_sheet.png` + `fig_index.json` (320 sprites): base body in five skins (natural and augmented), every DollSpec outfit and worn layer (`t64_kit.py`), a held sprite for every gun, melee weapon and tool (`t64_held.py`), 7 hair styles x 7 colours and 4 eye shapes x 6 colours (`t64_hair.py`); not yet drawn in game.
- Standing dolls in game (2026-10-18): every person is drawn as a standing doll (`src/figure.gd` loads the atlas, `DollSpec.figure_keys` picks the layers: body by skin, augmented body when an implant shows, sleeves cover the arms, the held item, hair and eyes; mirrored when facing left); the top-down tileset dolls remain the fallback.  Creation offers skin tone, hair style, hair colour, eye shape (new `app.eye_shape`, old saves get round) and eye colour, with the standing doll as the preview; the body-frame row is hidden until a second standing body exists.
